# IELTS Reading — 4-Week Plan (target: 6.5 overall, no band < 6.0)

**Made for:** Canadian M.A.Sc. applicant — **cost: $0**, uses only official free materials.
**Syncs with:** `reading_timer.html` (offline phone tracker) — score every drill there.

## Where you are → where you need to be

| Metric | Now (honest) | Target (safe for M.A.Sc.) |
|---|---|---|
| Raw Reading score | 18-23 / 40 | **27-30 / 40** |
| Approx band | 5.5 - 6.0 | **7.0** |

Your cushion: you only need a **6.5 overall with no band below 6.0**. Grammar so
our target is deliberately ABOVE the minimum so a bad Writing day can't sink you.
**+7 to +10 raw points in 4 weeks is very realistic** — Reading is the fastest
section to improve because it's technique, not lifetime vocabulary.

## The core rule — always timed, always scored

Never do a passage "just reading." Every single session = **one passage, 20-minute
timer** (real test gives 20 min/passage), then score /40 on the tracker. Speed +
accuracy grow together. Reading untimed teaches you nothing about test day.

## Week-by-week

### Week 1 — Find your wrecking ball (foundation)
- **Days 1-7:** ONE timed 20-min passage per day from official Academic Reading PDFs.
- **Do AFTER each passage:** mark every wrong answer and CODE it:
  - `V` = didn't know a word (vocab) · `K` = knew the word but missed the keyword match ·
  - `T` = ran out of time / rushed · `NG` = misjudged TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN · `M` = misread the question type
- **Goal by Sunday:** know your top 2 error codes. That's your whole plan from here.
- **Bonus:** 10 min daily — read ONE news article, then without looking, summarize aloud what the article's opinion is (trains "paragraph = one main idea" instinct).

### Week 2 — Kill the top error code (technique)
- **Same timed passage daily**, but now with ONE drill each day from the list, matched
  to your #1 error from Week 1:
  - **Headings matching:** read only paragraph first+sentence, skim the rest. After, recheck.
  - **True/False/Not Given:** `only/all/never/always` words usually = FALSE; information never mentioned = NOT GIVEN (NOT the opposite of what's said!).
  - **Sentence completion / blanks:** answer is a noun or number COPYED from the text; respect word limits ("NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS").
  - **Multiple choice:** eliminate 2 obviously-wrong, then pick using EXACT words in the passage.
- **Goal by Sunday:** raw score climbing — any passage ≥ 24/40.

### Week 3 — Speed reprocessor (time pressure)
- Now do the passage **first read scan = 3 minutes max** (just first sentence of each
  paragraph + each paragraph's first + last lines), THEN dive into questions.
- **Technique swap this week:** answer questions in a different order — do the "answers
  come in passage order" types (blanks, TFNG, sentence completion) BEFORE headings matching.
- **Friday = full mock:** one full Reading test (3 passages, 60 min, official PDF) timed, scored /40. Log it on the tracker.

### Week 4 — Match day conditions (consolidation + nerves)
- **Mon-Wed:** one timed passage + review, keeping the Week-2/3 habits.
- **Thu:** FULL timed Reading mock (60 min, all 3 passages, official paper).
- **Fri-Sat:** only LIGHT review — skim your error-code log, do one short passage untimed
  to keep confidence, NO heavy drills (rest sharpens you).
- **Sun = TEST DAY. Do not do a full mock within 24h.** Eat, sleep, breathe.
